Theory of labor markets: input pricing

Derived demand

Demand for labor is derived from demand for the final product and shaped by prevailing market conditions.

Production

Firms choose input combinations—including medical services—to produce health.

Q = Q(X, Y, …, Z)

Assume a production process and competitive input choices.

Equilibrium condition

MPX/PX = MPY/PY = … = MPZ/PZ
PX/MPX = … = PZ/MPZ = MC = MR
PX = MPX × MR
PY = MPY × MR

MP = marginal product; P = price; MC = marginal cost; MR = marginal revenue. The prices of X and Y equal their marginal revenue products.

Do physicians respond to incentives?

Financial incentives

Key to explaining lower levels of spending and utilization.

Physicians at risk of budget deficits spent less per enrollee, largely through lower specialist referral costs.
